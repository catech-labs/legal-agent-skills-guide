# v0.1.0 — Initial public guide

Released: 2026-08-03

## What is included

- independent comparison of five leading open legal Agent Skills projects;
- recommendations by use case rather than popularity alone;
- English and Brazilian Portuguese documentation;
- explanation of skills, prompts, agents, and MCP servers;
- installation examples;
- security, confidentiality, jurisdiction, licensing, and human-review checklist;
- structured YAML catalog;
- contribution, security, support, conduct, governance, and ownership policies;
- roadmap for Brazilian legal skills and future benchmarks.

## Recommended audience

- legal professionals evaluating open AI workflows;
- legal operations and innovation teams;
- developers integrating Agent Skills;
- researchers studying legal AI tooling;
- organizations designing controlled legal-agent environments.

## Known limitations

- the guide does not certify listed projects;
- recommendations are based primarily on public upstream documentation;
- project metadata and scope can change after the review date;
- Brazilian legal coverage remains an explicit roadmap item;
- automated link, license, activity, and security checks are not yet implemented.

## Upgrade policy

Future releases will document recommendation changes, additions, removals, and methodology updates in `CHANGELOG.md`.
