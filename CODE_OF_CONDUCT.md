# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- critique ideas and evidence, not people;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid sharing confidential, privileged, personal, or client information;
- distinguish factual findings from opinions and recommendations;
- accept correction when project information becomes outdated.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- publishing private information without permission;
- submitting malicious code, hidden instructions, credential-stealing logic, or unsafe installation steps;
- misrepresenting a project as verified, certified, secure, or legally accurate without evidence;
- using issues or pull requests for unsolicited promotion unrelated to the guide.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or lock contributions and conversations that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

## Reporting

For ordinary moderation issues, open an issue without including sensitive data. For security-sensitive matters, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant and tailored to a legal-technology research repository.
